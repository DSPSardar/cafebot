About Us

The Cozy Cup started as a small counter with three stools and a big idea: coffee should taste like it was made just for you. Today we're still small, still independent, and still roasting our beans with the same care as day one.

Come sit a while, catch up with a friend, or just enjoy a quiet moment with a good cup of coffee.
